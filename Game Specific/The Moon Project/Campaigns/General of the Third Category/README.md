New Campaign: UCS Campaign by KP2140

Dive into a brand-new 13-mission campaign featuring an intriguing plot that follows the UCS’s relentless push to crush LC and ED forces. Crafted by KP2140 from the Polish community, this campaign puts you right in the boots of a despised and humiliated UCS General—meaning UCS High Command certainly won't be treating you with any kid gloves! ;)

English Questions / Comments: Please direct them to Rosic for translation.

What to Expect:
- Massive Warfare: Prepare for huge, satisfying battles involving dozens of vehicles, alongside a dedicated mission that lets you put space ships through their paces.
- Diverse Objectives: Missions range from all-out destruction and base repairs to setting up tactical ravine defenses to hold off the enemy.
- Challenging & Rewarding: Features a gratifyingly high difficulty level paired with newly crafted maps and unique, tongue-in-cheek briefing orders from High Command.
- Authentic Lore: The storyline ties closely into the official Earth canon, offering a fresh, alternative perspective on the war of 2150.

As the first major mod from the Polish community, The Moon Project is truly getting a second wind. It’s well worth the download—jump in and check it out! :>
